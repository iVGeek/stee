FAQ

Questions, answered

Is everything I say confidential?

Yes. Everything we discuss stays between us, with the limited legal and ethical exceptions of harm to yourself or others.

What should I expect in my first session?

We'll get to know each other, talk about what brought you in, and agree on goals. There's nothing to prepare — just come as you are.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes, by secure video or phone, worldwide. Pick "Online session" when booking and you'll receive your private link before the session.

How does payment work?

You pay securely by card or bank transfer through Paystack when booking. Prefer to talk first? Use "Pay later on WhatsApp" and we'll arrange it.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule up to 24 hours before your session at no charge. Sessions cancelled with less notice are charged at 50%.

Is this a crisis or emergency service?

No — this website is not a crisis line. If you are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line right away.
