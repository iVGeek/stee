FAQ

Questions, answered

Is everything I say confidential?

Yes. Everything we discuss stays between us, with the limited legal and ethical exceptions of harm to yourself or others.

How long are sessions?

Individual sessions run for about 50 minutes and couples sessions for about 75 minutes.

How often will we meet?

Most clients begin with weekly sessions so we can build momentum. We'll adjust the frequency as you progress.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes, by secure video or phone. Pick the online option when booking and you'll receive your private link before the session.

How does payment work?

Pay by card or M-Pesa when booking. Prices are shown in USD at live exchange rates and billed in Kenyan Shillings. Flexible payment plans are available — just ask.

Is this a crisis or emergency service?

No — this website is not a crisis line. If you are in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services right away. For non-urgent concerns I'll get back to you within 24 hours.
